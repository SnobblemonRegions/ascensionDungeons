# Ascension Dungeons changelog

## 0.0.9-open-graveyard (2026-11-23)

### New
- Outbreak dives: every type dungeon is now a timed push-or-extract run. The dive lasts 45 minutes and the Outbreak rises a stage every 6 minutes, bringing more enemies and spells. What you carry but have not banked is at risk.
- The Depths: a large maze beneath every dive, different on each run, reached by a tunnel and stairway. It is split into Outer, Middle and Deep bands. Deeper bands hold luckier chests and tougher waves, and the band's name shows on screen as you cross into it.
- The extraction portal is always open, but leaving costs something: fight the gatekeeper, or pay a 35 percent toll on your unbanked loot. A window shows exactly what the toll would take before you pay.
- Your first extraction is free, and your first knock-out keeps half of what you had not banked. Both happen once per player, for good.
- /dive opens a picker of the type dungeons, gentlest first, with each one's tier, enemy levels and length. The gentlest is marked as the one to start with. Parties go in by invitation with /dive invite, accept, leave and party, and the leader picks the difficulty.
- A first-dive tutorial with lessons, a Guide bar and a dive guide.
- Pokemon can now turn up in dive chests.
- Chests have their own models for each kind of loot.
- Enemies fight with tactics at four difficulties: Casual, Normal, Hard and Nightmare. Difficulty changes how enemies fight, not their damage, health or rewards.

### Changed
- Dive enemies cannot be caught.
- Dungeon chests are Lootr chests, so every player gets their own loot.
- Dives use real-time combat.
- The tunnel, stairway and maze passages are wider so every Pokemon in a dungeon's pool fits through them.
